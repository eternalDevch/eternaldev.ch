Portfolio

Overview

A personal portfolio website designed to showcase my development and design projects, built as my first university project, the layout follows the Swiss / International Typographic Style, using a strict grid system, high contrast, a restrained color palette, and no decorative elements without function, projects are presented as compact cards on the main view, selecting one opens a detailed breakdown covering role, stack, and a structured walkthrough of goal, approach, and result, alongside supporting visuals, beyond showcasing work the site includes a contact pathway combining a direct contact form with email and social links, positioning it as a single destination for both portfolio content and outreach.

Mission

Make direct contact effortless. The site exists so people can reach me fast and simple. Portfolio content supports this mission it, giving visitors enough context to know who they are contacting and why before they do it. As my first university project, the mission stays the constant as more work gets added over time, contact first, everything else built around it.

Stack

Approach

Strict grid system as the structural baseline. High contrast, near black background, restrained palette, no decorative elements without a functional role. Typography carries the hierarchy instead of imagery or color. Fast load times follow as a natural consequence of the minimal approach rather than a deliberate separate target.
