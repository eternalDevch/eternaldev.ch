General

This website is operated as a personal portfolio. The protection of your personal data is important to me. This privacy policy informs you about what data is collected when you visit this website and how it is handled. The applicable law is the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Hosting & Server Logs

This website is hosted by an external provider. When you access the site, technical data is automatically recorded in server logs: IP address, date and time of access, requested URL, browser and operating system used, and the referring website (referrer). This data is technically necessary for the operation of the website and is not linked to any other data.

Contact Form

When you use the contact form, the data you provide (name, email address, message) is collected and processed for the purpose of handling your inquiry. This data is used exclusively to respond to your message and is not passed on to third parties. Once communication is complete, the data is deleted unless a statutory retention obligation applies. Use of the contact form constitutes your voluntary consent to the processing of this data.

Tracking and Analytics

This website uses tracking and analytics tools to understand usage and improve the service over time. Anonymized usage data is collected, such as pages visited, time spent, and device type. This website also uses Google Fonts, which may transmit your IP address to Google when the page loads. No cookies are set for advertising purposes.
