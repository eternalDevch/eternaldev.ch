General

This website is operated as a personal portfolio. The protection of your personal data is important to me. This privacy policy informs you about what data is collected when you visit this website and how it is handled. The applicable law is the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Hosting & Server Logs

This website is hosted by ywt.ch (Switzerland). When you access the site, technical data is automatically recorded in server logs by the hosting provider: IP address, date and time of access, requested URL, browser and operating system used, and the referring website (referrer). This data is technically necessary for the operation and security of the website and is not linked to any other data.

DNS & Content Delivery (Cloudflare)

This website uses Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) for DNS management and network security. Depending on the routing configuration, connection data (such as your IP address and technical request headers) may pass through Cloudflare's network to ensure reliable and secure delivery of the website. Where data is transferred to the USA, Cloudflare is certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Fonts & External Resources

All fonts used on this website are hosted locally on the web server. No requests are made to external font providers (such as Google Fonts), and no data is transmitted to third parties for typography rendering.

Contact Form

When you use the contact form, the data you provide (name, email address, message) is collected and processed for the purpose of handling your inquiry. This data is used exclusively to respond to your message and is not passed on to third parties. Once communication is complete, the data is deleted unless a statutory retention obligation applies. Use of the contact form constitutes your voluntary consent to the processing of this data.

Tracking, Analytics & Cookies

This website does not use third-party tracking or analytics tools, and no cookies are set for advertising or profiling purposes.

Your Rights

Under the Swiss FADP, you have the following rights with respect to the responsible person:

  • Access to the personal data stored about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion or restriction of processing
  • Objection to processing

Requests can be directed to: info@eternaldev.ch

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

External Links

This website may contain links to external websites (such as GitHub or social profiles). I accept no responsibility for their content or data protection practices.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated at any time. The current version is always available on this page.
