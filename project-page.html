FuelSplit

Development

A fuel calculator built for Le Mans Ultimate. Takes lap time, fuel/lap, and race duration as inputs to calculate exactly what you need for a stint or a full race. Built out of a real need within the team to plan fuel strategy without manual recalculation during a race weekend, precise enough to cut pit stop guesswork down to the second. Currently working on in-game integration.
